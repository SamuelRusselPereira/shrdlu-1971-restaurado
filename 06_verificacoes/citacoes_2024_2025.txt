- Eric Swenson, 04/08/2024: "They were mostly complete and certainly not lost." / "We got the sources from the Tapes of Tech Square (ToTS) archive at MIT."
  (fala dos fontes de 1973-74, hoje em src/shrdlu do PDP-10/its)
- Eric Swenson, 04/08/2024: "The SHRDLU demo, which we have had committed to the ITS GitHub repository does not have any accompanying sources.
  In fact, however, I do have sources for that demo, but have not yet tried building the demo from those sources."
- Eric Swenson, 04/08/2024: "So the demo (TWDEMO) is nothing more than a scripted presentation that renders on the Type 340 display."
- Charley Jones, 05/08/2024: "Sadly, the original interactive Shrdlu has not been recovered. We have only the scripted demo."
- Eric Swenson, 05/08/2024: "Actually, the original SHRDLU *has* been recovered." (idem: os fontes de 1973-74)
- Eric Swenson, 05/08/2024, relatando conversa com Terry Winograd: TWDEMO "was a scripted movie driver that output to the Type 340 display",
  "all commands and responses had been run through SHRDLU", "he may have updated SHRDLU multiple times to bluff bugs", e o roteiro foi criado a partir de
  "interactions with SHRDLU prior to a complete rewrite by Stu Card in 1971 or 1972".
- Eric Swenson, 03/12/2025: "None of the sources you see in the SHRDLU directory (save DEMO FLICK) have anything to do with TWDEMO"
- Eric Swenson, 04/12/2025: "Yes it is. I wish we had source to TWDEMO, but we don't."
- Lars Brinkhoff, 11/12/2025: "I haven't examined dumped LISP images much, but if I recall they are hard to understand."

Observacoes:
- Sobre o fonte do TWDEMO, Swenson disse em ago/2024 que tinha fontes da demo (sem ter tentado compilar) e em dez/2025 que nao ha fonte do TWDEMO. Nenhum fonte do TWDEMO foi publicado. O que existe e o roteiro (demo.flick) e o binario (ts.twdemo).
- Quando Swenson diz que "the original SHRDLU has been recovered", fala dos fontes de 1973-74 das fitas ToTS (pasta 01 deste repositorio), nao da versao da epoca da tese.
- O relato de Winograd (via Swenson) de uma reescrita completa por Stu Card em 1971 ou 1972 e coerente com o que este repositorio mostra: uma versao anterior a reescrita (arquivos Z, moby dump) e a versao reescrita (fitas ToTS, 1973-74).
