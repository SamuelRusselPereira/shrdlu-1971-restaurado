This MIT license covers the new work in this repository: the scripts, the
runtime fixes (*.lisp files in 10_moby_rodando/), the analyses, comparisons,
transcripts and texts written in 2026 (folders 06_verificacoes/,
09_moby_dump_extraido/, 10_moby_rodando/, README.md and the documentation files).
